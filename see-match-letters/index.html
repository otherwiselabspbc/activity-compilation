About this activity

Corpus entry ORT-01. Orthographic Knowledge › Tactile Only › See, Match › See Letter, Touch Matching Letter.

Units. Letter Units, Group 1 (lower-case a–z) or Group 2 (upper-case A–Z), one case per run. Each run uses 3–7 letters.

The exercise. The child sees a letter at the top and touches the letter below that is the same. The other letters are look-alikes for that letter (b with d and p, m with n), so the child has to look at the letter's parts, not just its outline. No letter name or sound is ever spoken: this is a looking task, a short warm-up for naming and sound work.

Scoring. The first touch on each item is the score (correct or not). A wrong first touch is shown and explained, the child touches the match, and the letter comes back later in the run. Because guessing is easy with two to four choices, mastery needs 90% or better over at least 10 items across two sessions; this page reports items and does not decide mastery.

What it reports. Per item: the letter, the letters shown and where, which one was touched, the kind of mix-up (reversal such as b for d, look-alike, or other), how quickly, whether help came first, and whether a run of fast mistakes slowed the pace.
